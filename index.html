Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any tools or technical skills to set it up?+
None at all. Every lamp ships fully assembled with the electrical cord already attached. You just screw in an E26 LED bulb (included) and plug it in. That's it.
Can I choose a specific lamp I see on this website?+
Yes! Click any lamp to open its product page, note the design name (e.g. "Aurora Spiral — SKU-104855"), then mention it in the order notes on our Etsy store. We'll print that exact design for you.
What if the color looks different in person than on screen?+
Screen colors vary, so we offer a 14-day return/exchange policy. If the color isn't exactly what you expected, contact us and we'll make it right — no questions asked.
How fragile are the lamps?+
Printed in PLA+ or PETG, our lamps are more durable than they look. Normal household use — moving, dusting, occasional bumps — is no problem. We wouldn't recommend dropping them from height, but they're designed for real living spaces.
Can I order a lamp as a gift?+
Absolutely. Add a gift message in the order notes and we'll include a handwritten card. We also offer gift wrapping — just ask when ordering.
